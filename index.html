A little souk, right by the sea.

Mini Souk Agadir is a pocket-sized take on the Moroccan market: the colors, the craft and the curiosity, without the crowds. We gathered what a day on the Agadir coast actually calls for and put it under one cool roof.

Pop in after a swim, grab a book for the sand, try an argan oil that was pressed nearby, and leave with something that feels like the place. No rush. Sandy feet are welcome.
